Words used in this lab

Context window
One shared budget for everything you send plus the reply Claude writes. Your system prompt, the conversation so far, your tool definitions and the answer all come out of the same allowance.
max_tokens
The most tokens the reply may use. A ceiling, not a target. Setting it high does not make answers longer, but it does reserve room inside the context window.
stop_reason
A field on the response saying why Claude stopped writing. Check this field rather than reading the text to work out what happened.
Prompt caching
Paying less for a large piece of text you send unchanged on many requests. It only helps if that text sits at the very start of the request and does not change.
Model tier
The family of models from fastest and cheapest through to most capable. Choosing one is a trade between cost, speed and quality.
